About me

Born in Turkey, I've lived in the USA, Sweden, and Finland before settling in the Netherlands in 2014. When I'm not designing or facilitating workshops, I'm usually surfing, exploring new places, or bringing people together around design and innovation.

When it comes to products, I like Spotify for the way it enhances both my solo moments and time with friends, and I dislike plastic shoehorns because they rarely do the one simple task they were designed for.
